# CineWeave Demo Script

---

## Opening Hook (30 seconds)

> "Every AI video tool today makes you a passive user. You type a prompt, you get a video. That's it.
>
> But that's not how cinema works. Cinema is a *pipeline*. A director has an idea, a creative lead makes decisions, and then — video, music, narration all come together under a coherent vision.
>
> Today, AI tools are isolated. You use one model to write, another to generate video, another for music — and they're all talking past each other.
>
> CineWeave changes that."

---

## Introducing the Interface (1 minute)

> "This is CineWeave — a visual cinematic workflow studio.
>
> On the left, you have your node library. This is where you pick the AI models you want in your pipeline.
>
> In the center — the canvas. Think of it like a node graph, like Unreal's Blueprints or TouchDesigner. You're designing *how intelligence flows*.
>
> On the right — the Inspector. Click any node, configure it.
>
> At the top — the toolbar. Here's where you run your workflow and watch it execute in real time."

---

## The Pipeline (1 minute)

> "Let me open the demo workflow.
>
> This is the **Cinematic Trailer** template. Watch what happens.
>
> The user starts with an **Idea** — a raw creative concept.
>
> That idea flows into **M3** — MiniMax M3. This is our Creative Director. M3 doesn't just 'improve your prompt.' It *reasons* about the creative vision. It decides story direction, tone, emotional arc, scene breakdown — even camera direction.
>
> M3 outputs what we call a **Cinematic Blueprint** — a structured creative document.
>
> From that blueprint, three things happen in parallel:
> - **H3** generates the video footage
> - **Music 3.0** composes the score
> - **Speech 2.8** creates narration
>
> All three flow into the **Final Output** node — your cinematic result.
>
> The key insight: *M3 is the reasoning layer.* It connects creative decision-making to generative outputs. That's the pipeline."

---

## Live Demo — Click "Run Workflow" (2-3 minutes)

> "Let me run this.
>
> Click **Run Workflow**.
>
> Watch the Idea node activate first — it's ready.
>
> M3 kicks in. This is where the reasoning happens. M3 is receiving the idea, reasoning about it, and producing the Cinematic Blueprint.
>
> You can see the processing message updating in real time. M3 doesn't just generate a prompt — it's making creative decisions. Genre, tone, emotional arc, scene structure.
>
> Once M3 completes, it passes structured outputs to H3 and Music simultaneously.
>
> H3 is now generating your video. It received a *precisely crafted prompt from M3* — not a raw user input. That's important. The prompt has lineage.
>
> Music 3.0 is composing in parallel — receiving the music brief from M3's blueprint.
>
> [If polling takes a moment]
>
> These are async jobs. CineWeave polls in real time, updating each node as results come in.
>
> [When complete]
>
> There it is — your video, your music, your narration, all generated and assembled.
>
> And if you want to see *how* it was made — click **View Lineage**."

---

## The Killer Feature — Lineage (45 seconds)

> "Every generated asset in CineWeave has a production lineage. You can see exactly how it was made.
>
> User Idea → M3 Creative Direction → M2.7 Prompt Optimization → H3 Video Generation → Assembled Output.
>
> This proves CineWeave isn't making isolated API calls. It's running a *connected creative intelligence system.*
>
> When you generate music, you can trace it back — the score was composed based on M3's music brief, which was derived from the emotional arc M3 defined, which came from the original user idea.
>
> That's *reasoning driving generation*."

## Tips for Delivery

- **Pause after "But that's not how cinema works."** Let it land.
- **When running the demo, narrate what the audience should watch** — "Watch the Idea node first... now M3 activates..."
- **If the API is slow**, narrate: "This takes a moment because we're generating actual video — not just text."
- **Emphasize "lineage"** — it's the strongest differentiator. Say it like you mean it.
- **Keep energy up** — this is a demo, not a lecture.
